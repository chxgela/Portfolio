About me

A closer look at who I am and what I enjoy building.

I'm Angela Andal Dalangin, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Batangas State University – The National Engineering University, Lipa Campus.

What keeps me interested in IT is watching a real problem turn into something people can actually use. My projects so far have come from the places around me: a community in Lipa City, households in Barangay Balintawak, and students on my own campus.

I'm still learning, and I learn best by building. Each project pushes me to connect what I study, from web development and databases to system analysis and UI/UX, into one working system.
